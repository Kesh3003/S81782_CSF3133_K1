About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot in 1979, providing facilities for students and lecturers from the fisheries and marine science programs to conduct practical sessions and research.

Following the restructuring of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia into Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), the campus became the UPM Terengganu branch campus in 1996. On 5 May 2001, the Malaysian government established it as an independent university college named Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) to focus on science, technology, and marine studies.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was officially upgraded to a full-fledged public university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT). Today, UMT is recognized as a leading institution in marine science, oceanography, fisheries, maritime management, and sustainable resource technology.
