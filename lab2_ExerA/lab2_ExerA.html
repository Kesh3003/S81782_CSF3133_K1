About the Event

Welcome to the Annual Faculty Hackathon! This full-day intensive event brings together students across all computing disciplines to solve real-world problems through collaborative design, teamwork, and software innovation.

Featured Speaker: Dr. Abdul Khaleed

Dr. Abdul Khaleed is an industry solutions architect and AI researcher. He will deliver the morning keynote address covering modern decentralized systems, enterprise application design, and real-time data processing.

Featured Activity: Rapid Prototyping Sprint

Teams will have four hours to brainstorm, wireframe, and build a functioning prototype around an announced problem statement. Dedicated faculty mentors and technical advisors will provide continuous on-site guidance.
